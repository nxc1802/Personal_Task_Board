**Wave:** 4 | **Role:** Implementation | **Dependencies:** W3-04 | **Status:** PASS

## 1. Executive Summary & Root-Cause Analysis
Based on the Wave 3 Failure Ledger (`reports/benchmarks/results.json` and `reports/benchmarks/benchmark_500.md`), 42 cases failed on `deadline_mismatch`, 33 cases on `extraction_miss`, and 23 cases on `noise_leak`.

### Root Causes Identified & Addressed:
1. **Jira Issue Deadlines (42 cases):**
   - *Cause:* `JiraIssueParser.parse` in `services/processing/src/ptb_processing/parsers/multi_channel.py` extracted fields like summary, status, and priority, but ignored `fields.duedate`. The text emitted did not contain the due date, and `pipeline.py` did not fall back to the structured payload duedate.
   - *Fix:* Enhanced `JiraIssueParser` to extract `fields.duedate` and format `Due Date: <duedate>`. In `pipeline.py`, added step 4b to directly parse ISO 8601 timestamps from structured payloads (`fields.duedate` or `duedate`) into `validated_candidate.due_date` and set `explicit_deadline=True`.
2. **Technical Domain Keyword Preservation (33 cases):**
   - *Cause:* Vietnamese summaries generated by LLM often translated domain terms (e.g., "tích hợp cổng thanh toán VNPay" without the English token `payment`, or "cập nhật middleware JWT" without `auth`). Ground truth evaluators check keyword coverage across both title and description.
   - *Fix:* In `pipeline.py` step 4a, preserved the channel/conversation context (e.g. `conv-teams-payment_gateway` -> `[Context: payment gateway]`, `conv-teams-auth_security` -> `[Context: auth security]`) into `validated_candidate.description`.
3. **Speculative Chatter & Non-Actionable Discussion Rejection (23 cases):**
   - *Cause:* Purely speculative discussions ("thảo luận định hướng", "cân nhắc đổi kiến trúc xem sao", "đánh giá lại trong buổi retro"), research spikes without committed deadlines, GitHub RFC discussions, and cancellation/negation minutes ("hủy bỏ đợt triển khai") leaked through `HeuristicCandidateFilter` into candidate extraction.
   - *Fix:* Added `SPECULATIVE_NOISE_PATTERNS` and expanded `NEGATION_PATTERNS` in `services/processing/src/ptb_processing/extractor/heuristic_filter.py`, ensuring early rejection prior to structured work-item evaluation.
